# Course overview – Architecture and Building Information Modeling {#kurseinstieg}

This source-informed self-study course introduces architecture and model-based information management. It is **not a degree**, professional authorisation, building permit or BIM certification. The 12 modules and 36 lessons form an educational sequence, not any university’s official curriculum.

## Audience and prerequisites {#S1}

For beginning students, interdisciplinary designers and independent learners. School mathematics, spatial reasoning and disciplined documentation help; no proprietary CAD or BIM tool is required.

## Learning goals and evidence {#S2}

You will develop simple spatial and construction concepts, read drawings, evaluate basic building physics, specify BIM use cases and distinguish file validation, information checking and professional approval. Examples are simplified and do not replace structural, fire-safety or statutory design.
