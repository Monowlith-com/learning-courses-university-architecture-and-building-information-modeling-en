# Study path – Architecture and Building Information Modeling {#study-path}

## Getting started {#S1}

M1–M3 cover design briefs, place, access and representation. Produce a small room programme, site sketch and consistent plan/section drawings. Use exercises as checkpoints.

## Construction and environment {#S2}

M4–M6 connect load paths, component performance and indoor comfort to climate response and material quantities. For every figure, record units, assumptions and model boundaries. Structural, fire, accessibility and regulatory issues require external professional checking.

## Digital models and openBIM {#S3}

M7–M10 move from geometry and coordinates through information requirements and execution planning to IFC, IDS, clashes and BCF. Exercises may use any suitable software or small tabular mock models; verifiable information delivery is the learning outcome.

## Capstone and optional profile {#S4}

M11–M12 extend the pathway through quantities, sequence, operation and an integrated project. Submit a short report with design, model assumptions, sources, open technical issues and self-checks. This course awards no credits and does not qualify the learner for professional registration.
